# Mission-screening maps and streamed lunar detail

User requests: togglable map gradient combining sunlight, longest darkness, regional slope and Earth visibility on polar and whole Moon maps; an ice layer; Cesium ion close-up lunar tiles; lower startup and interaction latency. Existing map controls, NASA appearance, modes, data authority and offline behavior remain.

Landing suitability is a date-dependent regional screening index, never a landing approval or probability of success. Four equal default weights can be inspected and changed. Expose each physical quantity and units, its normalization and data resolution. Unknowns stay unknown, excluded spatial support is transparent, and a legend distinguishes lower/higher suitability from missing data. Regional slope cannot resolve gear-footprint hazards. Earth visibility is geometric, not a booked ground link. Click an overlay point to inspect its actual coordinates and current inputs through the existing scenario workflow. Smooth display must not invent unsupported spatial precision.

Ice is a separate evidence layer: prioritize published M3 detections/PSR evidence with explicit source, observation method, footprint and uncertainty. No hydrogen-to-confirmed-ice or concentration/reserve inference. If a source is merely a proxy it must have that explicit name; it must not masquerade as measured ice. Do not invent locations. Include primary references and maintainable provenance.

Streaming: retain Three.js and stable local/body frames, use Cesium Moon 3D Tiles asset2684829 (verified official Cesium docs), view-driven progressive refinement, bounded downloads/GPU caches, proper attribution and cancellation. No terrain tile may change the canonical numerical assessment or silently redefine collision/height datum. Ion authentication is currently pending in the connected browser. No credentials in source/export/offline. Offline retains measured bundled data; tiles are connected-mode only.

Performance: measure a cold load and first map/3D interaction before changes. Defer optional terrain/imagery/global data and heavy computations until needed; coalesce existing loaders; keep source/schema checks and visible unavailable/error states. Preserve the original3s loader requirement while making the app shell meaningful immediately and ready when it ends. Never lower numerical/visual fidelity silently to make a benchmark pass.

Verification: score invariants, date/height/mask/weight changes, missing data and poles/seam; ice source/projection/units/identity; cancellation/cache/frame/occlusion tiles and request budgets; web/file overlay interactions, clear/restore and no stale jobs; cold-load resource counts and timings; source/build/type/lint and final offline checks. Hosting remains teammate-owned.
