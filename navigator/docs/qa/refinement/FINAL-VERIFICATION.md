## Hardware observations

The final private-context benchmark observes approximately 55–60 completed renders/s during the named focus/site windows, about 46–48 ms for the edited value to reach the UI, and 59–62 ms to a completed render. The longest observed frame gap is 259.5 ms during Lander focus; these measurements do not promise zero stutter or a universal frame rate. Contact recalculation completes asynchronously in approximately 2.6–2.8 s in these cases while the model remains visible and framed. Site navigation intentionally zooms out and temporarily hides the tiny lander; the earlier benchmark mixed that phase into its edit window. Waiting for the completed destination and observing each completed edit render resolves that harness ambiguity without a product change.

See [hardware summary](evidence/c15/hardware-summary.json), [compressed full trace](evidence/c15/hardware-report.json.gz) and [benchmark method](gpu-performance.md). Source assets and local OS caches are warm; this is not a cold public-internet or target-device study.
