What makes this place work?

Connecting Ridge

Latitude changes how high the Sun can climb; near the poles it often skims the horizon. A ridge can hide the Sun or Earth even when a smooth Moon would leave them visible.

A different kind of day

Earth’s day is about 24 hours. A lunar sunrise-to-sunrise cycle averages about 29.5 Earth days; near the poles, low Sun angles and local terrain break that simple pattern. The next sunlight and Earth-visibility changes above come from this site and date, not that average.
