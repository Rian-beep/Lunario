# Qualified analytical engineering screens

These seven finite-input calculators add bounded engineering context. They do **not** close operational landing-readiness requirements. Inputs are empty by default; no measured site property, vehicle qualification, station booking or empirical plume bound is invented. Every successful analytical screen is `conditional`, never an unconditional pass. Parent integration retains separate qualified external assessments and binds supplied inputs/provenance to the scenario.

## Frozen API

`EngineeringDomain` is footprint, touchdown, trajectory, ground-support, thermal, plume or traverse. `EngineeringInputs = Partial<Record<EngineeringDomain,Record<string,number>>>`. `ENGINEERING_SCREEN_FIELDS` provides each domain's exact numeric IDs, labels, units, min/max and intuition. `ENGINEERING_EXTERNAL_IDS` maps these domains to existing landing considerations.

`parseEngineeringInputs(unknown)` returns a fresh normalized partial input or null. Missing values remain missing; unknown keys, strings/coercion, nonfinite numbers, fractions outside0–1, negative quantities, nonpositive divisors, final mass above initial mass, reversed thermal limits and cone half-angle≥90° are rejected. Empty/absent inputs normalize to an empty object; null is malformed. No persisted metric is accepted as an input.

`evaluateEngineeringScreens(inputs={})` returns seven `{id,title,state,metrics,assumptions,gaps,sources}` records. Metrics are `{label,value:number|null,unit}`; sources are `{label,url}`. Invalid input throws a readable RangeError. Missing or numerically unrepresentable calculations remain not-assessed/null rather than zero. A separately established known violation remains fail even with other missing inputs. No record can return pass. All records retain limitations and source roles.

## Models, inputs and validity

| Domain | Numerical screening | Required input/evidence and unresolved scope |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | Compare measured maximum slope, defined roughness, hazard fraction and source spacing against supplied limits; full footprint coverage is required for a conditional result. | Actual footprint measurements, coverage, matching roughness baseline/definition and vehicle-specific limits. Source spacing is not effective resolution. No regional DEM or image brightness creates local rock/soil measurements. |
| Touchdown | Vertical/horizontal/total kinetic energy; constant net vertical deceleration v²/(2s); ideal minimum stroke v²/(2aLimit); normal load factor1+a/g; static tipping boundary atan(minimum support half-width/COM height). | Supplied contact speeds, mass, effective stroke, allowable average net deceleration, support geometry, slope and gravity. Contact at the static tipping boundary fails. Lateral stopping, gear dynamics, structural peaks, bounce, attitude and soil/sinkage remain unresolved. |
| Trajectory | Ideal rocket delta-v=Isp×g0×ln(initial/final mass); circular and escape speeds; coplanar two-impulse Hohmann delta-v and half-ellipse time between supplied circular radii. | Explicit spherical radius and GM, orbit altitudes, maneuver masses, Isp and complete user delta-v requirement. Budget is checked against both the supplied requirement and ideal transfer necessity. No complete descent/ascent, finite-burn guidance, losses, plane change, terrain collision or navigation solution is generated. |
| Ground support | Allocated overlap=available overlap×allocated fraction. Data capacity=allocated hours×3600×usable Mbit/s. Compare duration and data against supplied requirements. | Joint station/spacecraft availability, allocated time and usable rate must already account for the intended link. Earth visibility alone supplies none of these. No station geometry, live schedule, RF margin, weather or handover qualification is inferred. |
| Thermal | One-node steady-state radiation balance, equilibrium temperature and heater power needed at the minimum permitted temperature. | All fluxes, optical properties, areas, incidence/view factors, internal heat and temperature/heater limits are supplied. No transient heat capacity, conduction, spatial gradients, multilayer geometry or component qualification. Heater electrical draw is **not** automatically added to mission load. |
| Plume | Geometric membership in a supplied empirical axial range/cone, with additional radial clearance. Boundary points count inside. | Applicable engine/regolith empirical angle/range, plume axis and asset coordinates. Missing clearance is not defaulted to zero; an inside-base-cone violation can still be established, while outside safety remains unknown. No plume flow, erosion, particle/ejecta range or safe separation is predicted. |
| Traverse | Explicit outbound+return route time and energy, including constant auxiliary load; compare remaining energy with final reserve and moving time with supplied allowance. | Supplied route lengths, speed, Wh/km, auxiliary load, initial usable energy, reserve fraction and available moving time. No route finding, mobility/soil model, work/stops, oxygen, thermal, rescue/walk-back or recharge is inferred. |

Thermal details: direct solar heat=absorptivity×projected area×solar flux×incidence; albedo heat=absorptivity×projected area×albedo flux×planet view factor; planetary IR heat=emissivity×radiating area×IR flux×planet view factor. The complementary sky view contributes emissivity×sigma×radiating area×(1−planet view factor)×background temperature⁴. Outgoing radiation is emissivity×sigma×radiating area×node temperature⁴. Equilibrium sets stored heat rate to zero. The heater deficit compares powers directly; this avoids introducing a physical-temperature tolerance through a fourth-root comparison. A cold result with enough supplied heater capacity remains conditional, not a survival certification.

`STANDARD_GRAVITY_MPS2=9.80665` is the terrestrial Isp conversion constant, not lunar surface gravity. `STEFAN_BOLTZMANN_W_M2_K4=5.670374419e-8` is the numerical representation used for radiation. Both are documented in [NIST's CODATA table](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt). R and GM are explicit user inputs; no hidden lunar constants or automatic site values are inserted.

Two pure bounded helpers are also exported. `supportOverlap(intervals,startHours,endHours)` clips at most256 finite intervals, integrates union duration and the **best single-link** capacity at overlaps, and does not sum duplicate windows into parallel radios. `traverseRoute(segments)` totals at most256 explicit distance/speed/drive-energy/auxiliary-load segments. Both reject invalid or overflowing results; empty supplied collections have a mathematical zero total, not a claim of measured mission support.

## Primary reference roles

- [NASA hazard-detection lidar](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hazard-detection-lidar/) and [ALHAT slope/roughness software](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110003001) establish why resolved local slope/rock measurements and hazard processing matter; they do not endorse a universal threshold entered here.
- [NASA landing-gear dynamic/static tests](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19640005067) demonstrate configuration, speed, attitude and surface dependence. The module's rigid static projection and constant-deceleration estimates are introductory mechanics, not reproduction or qualification of that tested vehicle.
- [NASA Glenn's ideal rocket equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/ideal-rocket-equation/) supplies the ideal propulsion basis; [NASA's trajectory chapter](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter4-1/) explains circular-endpoint Hohmann transfer and targeting limits. Standard two-body expressions are implemented with the supplied central-body parameters, not a complete mission optimizer.
- [NASA/JPL DSN810-005](https://deepspace.jpl.nasa.gov/dsndocs/810-005/) describes telecommunications interfaces and hardware performance. The capacity arithmetic here uses supplied usable rates/windows and grants no DSN allocation.
- [NASA thermal-control guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/thermal-control/) distinguishes solar, albedo, planetary IR, generated, stored and radiated heat. This simplified model omits the stored/transient and conductive terms explicitly.
- [NASA plume/surface interaction research](https://www.nasa.gov/plume-surface-interaction/) treats flow, erosion and ejecta as coupled modeling/testing problems. A supplied geometric keep-out region does not replace them.
- [NASA EHP ConOps RevB](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240011450) provides traverse/return-contingency context. Its role is operational context, not certification of the constant-speed/energy arithmetic or current mission rules.

All primary pages were browsed for this implementation. No source provides approval of this app, these entered assumptions or a particular lunar landing site.

## Validation and integration boundaries

Ten tests use clearly synthetic analytical inputs, not measured lunar sites. They cover empty/default behavior; strict field/range parsing; full/incomplete footprint coverage; known failures with missing inputs; kinetic/stroke/static-boundary identities; rocket/circular/escape/Hohmann relations and transfer symmetry; overlap union/capacity allocation; thermal equilibrium/heater deficits and flux/view terms; cone boundary classification; return energy/reserve/time; and overflow-to-unknown behavior. Approximate assertions only check floating-point mathematical identities, not physical uncertainty.
