# International mission history handoff

Revision4 contract; base `cb9c29f`. Export `INTERNATIONAL_MISSION_HISTORY` from `lib/mission-history/international.ts` and `MissionHistoryCard({record, mode})` from this directory. The shared record type and existing landing-site inventory are unchanged. The integrator owns joining the US records and placing this card in the existing inspector/story.

The dataset covers all **24 existing international IDs**, each exactly once: 14 Luna, four Chang’e, two Chandrayaan, two HAKUTO-R, SLIM and Beresheet. Intentional impactors, orbiters, candidate points, separate rover payloads and additional missions are not added. There are **36 unique primary reference URLs**. Every non-null sourced field resolves to its record’s sources. Historical assertions do not enter the scientific assessment.

The card defaults to compact summary and separate touchdown/science outcomes. Expand facts reveals identity, objectives, findings and achievements; nested disclosures expose exploration evidence and original references. Educator mode adds a mission-specific investigation prompt; Planner adds mission-specific limits on reusing historical evidence. Public mode explains significance. All use the supplied `mode`, without a new audience context. Dismiss leaves a reopen button; selecting a different record resets dismissal and expansion. Facts scroll within `min(55vh, 28rem)`, use existing theme variables and shared Button, and introduce no stylesheet, imagery, live selection fetch, persistent state or animation.

Evidence decisions and remaining gaps:

- **13 operator fields remain null** where reviewed histories establish a builder/programme but not a separate flight operator. **20 sponsor fields remain null**; a national mission label is not treated as proof of a particular funding arrangement. The four positive support entries are explicitly scoped: Luna 25’s federal programme, one contemporary NGK corporate-partner attribution for HAKUTO-R 1, SMBC’s naming partnership for HAKUTO-R 2, and Israel Space Agency support for Beresheet. None claims a complete donor list or money amount.
- **18 discovery fields and 11 innovation fields remain null**, with reasons. A successful landing, acquired data or returned sample is not automatically a scientific discovery. Failed surface programmes are distinguished from useful flight/orbital data. Luna 13’s camera failure, Luna 21’s navigation/dust limitations, Yutu’s mechanism fault, Chandrayaan-2’s independent orbiter, Luna 23’s failed sampling, and SLIM’s pinpoint/attitude distinction remain explicit.
- **23 traverse distances and all 24 coverage areas remain null.** Lunokhod 1’s 10,540 m is reported by Lavochkin and converted to 10.54 km; it does not create geometry. Lunokhod 2’s historical 36.2/37 km scopes and later revisions are not reconciled into one adopted total. There are **zero route geometries**. Every exploration summary still has source/context links. Stationary measurements, a drill’s depth and a flight path are not converted into surface distance or surveyed area.
- Russian Lavochkin/IKI, Chinese CNSA, Japanese JAXA/ispace and Hebrew SpaceIL/Israel Space Agency originals carry English paraphrase notes. ISRO and operator-authored English pages are labelled English without pretending translation. IKI’s `/en/node/1363` has Russian article text; its erroneous Luna 13 launch-date sentence is not used. NASA’s artifact table supplies the existing Soviet surface-event dates and uncertain-identification context. Home-country dates that cross UTC midnight are explained for Chang’e 5/6, Chandrayaan-2, SLIM and HAKUTO-R; no landing coordinates were copied or revised.
- Lavochkin’s August 2023 chronology is linked at **PDF page 7**. Its Luna 5/7/8 crashes, Luna 18 failure and Luna 23 overturn support those narrow claims; it is not used for detailed failure mechanisms, numerical performance or operational certification. Luna 23’s communication/no-sample/sideways evidence is the original LROC observation page. NASA’s Luna 25 crater association remains **likely**, not confirmed.
